About Us: Celebrate with Pride!

Welcome to the Lavender Gift Basket Shop, your destination for vibrant and thoughtfully curated gift baskets bursting with Queer Trans Pride! We believe that every celebration, big or small, deserves to be filled with joy, love, and a touch of fabulousness. We also believe in centering Trans Joy and celebrating the big and small moments in life.



Our Story:

The Lavender Gift Basket Shop was born from a desire to share pride and support the incredible talent within the Queer community. We noticed a need for gift options that truly reflected and celebrated LGBTQIA+ identities, while also making a positive impact. What started as a spark of an idea has blossomed into a passion for connecting you with unique, high-quality goods made with love and intention by fellow Queer entrepreneurs.



Our Mission:

At the heart of Lavender Gift Basket Shop is a commitment to:

Celebrate Pride: We aim to amplify Queer and Trans pride through our vibrant gift baskets.

Support Queer Businesses: We are dedicated to ethically sourcing every item in our baskets directly from Queer-owned businesses, with a strong focus on small-scale creators.

Share the Story: We believe in the power of connection. Each basket includes information about the origin of its contents and the inspiring individuals behind the businesses. You can also find more details about each product and its maker on our website.

Spread Joy (Responsibly): We strive to create delightful and memorable gifts while being mindful of ethical sourcing and supporting our community.



What You'll Find:

Our carefully curated gift baskets are filled with fun, unique, and ethically sourced items that proudly showcase Queer and Trans themes. From handcrafted treasures to delightful treats, each basket is designed to bring joy and create opportunities for self care.



Important Note: The wonderful items you find within our gift baskets are exclusively available from us as part of a complete basket. This allows us to focus on creating thoughtfully combined collections and directly support the makers in a meaningful way. Individual items are not available for separate purchase on our website but there are links to the individual businesses under each part of the total product.
